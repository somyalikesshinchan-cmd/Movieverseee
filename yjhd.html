YEH JAWAANI HAI DEEWANI

A movie very famous for its quotes and songs. Like the previous movie, it also explores the theme of friendships. It portrays an important message of how important it is to choose someone who loves you- not only the person you love(like Aditi did in this movie). How necessary it is to show maturity when it is needed because always being unserious is not the way to live your life. I really like this quote, said by Naina in this movie, "Kuch na kuch to chhut hi jata hai", that is you cannot take two contradicting decisions simultaneously. Bunny, who likes to travel the world and' finds it thrilling, is in love with Naina, who argues that there is a comfort in staying in your own place, your nation, the place you can call 'home'.

10/10 Movie. A must watch.
