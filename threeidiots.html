3 idiots

I haven't met a single student (of 10+ age) in India who has not watched this movie. This was the first movie about questioning the education system which I saw. I feel deeply connected to it and I strongly relate to a song in this movie named 'Give me some sunshine' like some of my peers and seniors. This movie also explores the theme of close friendship or brotherhood- how things feel a little less heavy and how life becomes a little bit more easier by the impact of good friends. Well imo there were 4 idiots in this movie, these 3 definitely plus Chatur hehe

10/10 movie. You HAVE to watch it atleast once!
