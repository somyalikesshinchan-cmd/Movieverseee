KAL HO NA HO

Now coming to this movie, starring Shahrukh Khan,Priti Zinta and Saif Ali Khan. Kal Ho Na Ho is really close to my heart because when I watched this movie for the first time, I didn't know it was foreshadowing my future. Anyways, it's serves as a reminder to just go run and hug your family,friends, basically all your close ones... or even a stranger because "Kya pata kal ye sama ho na ho?"

10/10 movie, a must watch.
