ZINDAGI NA MILEGI DOBARA

This movie literally taught me the difference between surviving and living. The importance of escaping your reality even for a tiny weeny period of time and just chill and do adventures in a whole different country... like SPAIN. I learnt how important it is to enjoy my life fully because YOU LIVE ONLY ONCE(YOLO).It also explores the theme of love and friendships-unexpected and lasting from a long time and tells us how the decision of marriage needs much more consideartion than just your love or a settled family. Basically I adopted a bit of my personality from this movie.

10/10 movie. You will love it!
