HOW TO LOSE A GUY IN 10 DAYS

This is my favourite rom-com movie. I cannot say that this will always be my favourite rom-com movie because I haven't watched many. The trope of this movie is grumpy x sunshine, when calm meets chaos. Both of them were together for their respective purposes but they eventually fall in love with each other.A reminder of how the right person always stays and love finds you unexpectedly in different ways.

10/10 movie. Recommended.
