TITANIC

This movie changed my perspective about life. I forever wish that I could forget everything about this movie and watch it all over again. I have watched this movie more than a hundred times and I am willing to do a hundred times more.I owe my heart, my soul and my mind to this movie. I cried buckets of tears after watching the ending. The ending,according to James Cameron, the director of this movie, is open that is we are free to interpret the ending as Rose's dream or her afterlife. Personally,I have always interpreted it as Rose's spirit going back to where her heart belonged- TITANIC. Some people think that the movie romanticizes cheating. Well I am not sure if it does.Anyways, if it wasn't for this movie, I couldn't have become the person I am now. 10/10 movie for me.

10/10 movie. HIGHLY RECOMMENDED!
